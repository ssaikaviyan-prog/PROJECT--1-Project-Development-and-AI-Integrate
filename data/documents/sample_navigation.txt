VISION-LANGUAGE AUTONOMOUS NAVIGATION SYSTEM - TECHNICAL KNOWLEDGE BASE
================================================================================

1. INTRODUCTION TO AUTONOMOUS NAVIGATION
Autonomous navigation enables mobile robots and robotic vehicles to traverse unstructured environments safely without human intervention. The system follows a continuous perception-action loop: Sense -> Understand -> Retrieve Knowledge -> Reason -> Decide -> Act. Key components include visual perception, range sensing, semantic spatial mapping, global path planning, and local collision avoidance.

2. SENSOR ARCHITECTURE & OBSTACLE DETECTION
- LiDAR Sensors: Light Detection and Ranging (LiDAR) uses pulsed laser beams to construct accurate 360-degree 3D point cloud maps. LiDAR is highly effective for obstacle detection, spatial depth measurement, and SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping). It operates reliably across varied lighting conditions.
- Vision-Language Cameras: RGB-Depth cameras capture high-resolution visual scenes, enabling object recognition, semantic classification, and visual place recognition. Combined with Gemini Vision AI, the robot can identify complex objects such as chairs, doors, people, and hazards.
- Ultrasonic & IMU Sensors: Ultrasonic sonar sensors provide short-range proximity safety checks (within 0.3m to 2.0m). Inertial Measurement Units (IMU) track angular velocity and heading direction (yaw, pitch, roll).

3. PATH PLANNING AND NAVIGATION LOGIC
Autonomous trajectory execution relies on a multi-tiered navigation stack:
- Local Path Decision Engine: Continuously evaluates forward clearance and flank distances. If an obstacle is detected directly ahead within 1.5 meters, the navigation engine executes avoidance maneuvers:
  * TURN LEFT: Executed when the left corridor offers greater clearance.
  * TURN RIGHT: Executed when the right corridor offers greater clearance.
  * MOVE FORWARD: Executed when the forward path is unobstructed for >= 3.0 meters.
  * STOP: Emergency safety halt triggered when an obstacle is within 0.8 meters or critical battery thresholds are reached.

4. RETRIEVAL-AUGMENTED GENERATION (RAG) & GEMINI AI REASONING
The AI Assistant integrates Gemini LLM reasoning with local ChromaDB vector storage. Document chunks are converted to 384-dimensional vector embeddings (`gemini-embedding-001`). When a query is asked, semantic similarity search retrieves the top relevant context snippets, enabling Gemini to generate precise answers grounded strictly in documented robotics facts with explicit file source citations.

5. SAFETY PROTOCOLS AND LIMITATIONS
- Emergency Stop: Hard real-time override halting motor motion when range sensors detect immediate collision risks.
- Environmental Constraints: Transparent glass surfaces, heavy fog, and extreme dynamic glare can degrade optical sensors, requiring sensor fusion with wheel encoders and ultrasonic proximity detectors.
